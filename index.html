FREQUENT INQUIRIES

The Coffee Dialogue

What makes your 3D customizer extraction true to reality?

Every parameter in our 3D customizer—from water temperature (93.5°C) and needle-valve pressure profiling to vessel ceramic thermal inertia—mirrors the exact barista recipes dialed in at our physical counter every morning.

Can I purchase whole beans for home brewing?

Yes. All of our micro-lot single origins are packaged in nitrogen-flushed, 100% compostable valve bags within 2 hours of roasting. Our baristas will gladly grind to your exact micron specification (Chemex, Aeropress, Espresso, French Press).

Do you cater to dairy-free and vegan guests?

Unconditionally. We make our own stone-milled organic Swedish oat milk and activated Sicilian almond milk fresh every morning, paired with vegan plant-based butter croissants and gluten-free pastries.
